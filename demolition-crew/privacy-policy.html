Privacy Policy

Demolition Crew · ReverseRain · Effective November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Demolition Crew (the "Game") on Android or iOS, why it is processed, who processes it and the choices you have. The Game is developed and published by ReverseRain ("ReverseRain", "we", "us" or "our").

Summary

  • No accounts. You never sign up, log in or give us your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your progress stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any server.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive, store or analyse player data ourselves.
  • You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy & legal, or delete all game data on your device.

1. Who we are

ReverseRain is an independent game studio based in India. For the limited personal information described in this policy, ReverseRain is the developer of the app, and Google is an independent controller of the data it collects through its advertising services. You can contact us at any time at support.reverserain@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, payment details or any other information that directly identifies you. The Game has no accounts, no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

To save your game, the app stores the following on your device: your progress (in-game cash, sites cleared, equipment, crew and upgrades), statistics (such as blocks smashed and truck trips), settings (sound, vibration, graphics quality and reminders), daily reward and mission status, and simple counters used to limit how often ads are shown. This information never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Your device's own backup service (for example Android backup) may include it in your personal device backups, under the terms of that service.

2.3 Information collected by Google for advertising

The Game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). When ads are requested and shown, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the IDFA (only if you allow tracking) and the IDFV;
  • IP address and the approximate location (country or city) derived from it;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type and app version;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were requested, shown, watched to the end or tapped;
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We only see aggregated reports in AdMob (for example the total number of ads shown) that do not identify you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How the information is used

  • To run the Game: saving and loading your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show ads: delivering optional rewarded ads and a limited number of ad breaks, limiting how often you see the same ad, and measuring ad performance.
  • Personalised ads: only with your consent where consent is required by law. If you do not consent, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Security and fraud prevention: detecting invalid ad activity, such as fake views or clicks.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, scheduling reminders on your device (see section 6).

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Ads that use your data for personalisation, and the storage of or access to information on your device for advertising, rely on your consent. Limited processing needed to deliver non-personalised ads, measure them and prevent fraud relies on legitimate interests in offering the Game for free and keeping its advertising secure. Saving your game on your device is necessary to provide the Game you asked to use.

4. Ads in the Game

The Game is free to play and is supported by ads. There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads: always optional and clearly labelled. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, which is granted only after the ad finishes.
  • Ad breaks: at most two per day, shown only at natural pauses (for example after a sale or between sites), never when the app opens or closes and never during your first minutes of play.

Ads are restricted to content suitable for a general audience (maximum rating PG), and sensitive categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol are blocked.

5. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the Game, Google's consent message asks whether ads may use your data. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising through the same Privacy options button when it is shown.
  • iOS tracking: on iOS the Game asks for permission before using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the Game under Settings → Reminders, or in your device settings.

Declining consent never stops you from playing. You may simply see ads that are less relevant to you, or fewer ads.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet access: to request and show ads and the consent message. The Game itself works offline.
  • Notifications (optional, asked once at a natural moment): for local reminders, for example when your daily reward is ready or your crew has finished its shift. Reminders are scheduled by the app on your device. No notification token or other data is sent to us or to a push service, and reminders are never sent between 10 pm and 9 am.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) and App Tracking Transparency (iOS): used by Google for advertising as described above.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers. The only third party that receives data through the Game is Google, as the provider of AdMob and its consent platform. We may disclose information if required by law, but because we do not hold player data ourselves, in practice we have nothing to disclose.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the Game in a way that provided personal information to an advertising partner, contact us at support.reverserain@gmail.com and we will help you remove it.

9. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it with Settings → Privacy & legal → Delete all my data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the Game. We hold no copy, so deleted progress cannot be recovered. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policy.

10. Security

Ad and consent data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Your game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but keeping data on your device and away from our own servers greatly limits the risk.

11. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Google relies on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in its privacy policy.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP): access, correct, erase or port your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority.
  • United States (for example the CCPA/CPRA in California): know what personal information is collected, request deletion or correction, opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): obtain information about processing, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and have grievances addressed by us and, if unresolved, by the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Brazil (LGPD) and other countries: similar rights under your local law.

How to exercise your rights. Most rights can be exercised directly in the Game: use Privacy options to change consent and Delete all my data to erase your progress. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You can also email us at support.reverserain@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Because we do not hold your data, we may direct your request to Google where appropriate.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The latest version is always available in the Game and at https://reverse-rain.github.io/demolition-crew/privacy-policy.html, with its effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the Game before it takes effect.
