Privacy Policy

Demolition Crew · Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Demolition Crew (the "Game"), developed by ReverseRain ("we", "us"), and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Your progress (cash, upgrades, settings, statistics) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders (for example when your daily reward is ready) locally on your device. They are created by the app itself; no notification token or other data is sent to us or to a push service. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data (for example crash logs from the ads SDK).

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. If you consent (or where consent is not required by law), ads may be personalised. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the Game are of two kinds: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and a maximum of two short ad breaks per day shown between activities.

3. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the Game you are asked, through Google's consent message, whether ads may use your data. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising through the same Privacy options button when it is shown.
  • Device settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • If you decline consent you can still play; you may see fewer or non-personalised ads.

4. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game in a way that provided personal information to an ad partner, contact us and we will help.

5. Retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it with Settings → Privacy & legal → Delete all my data or uninstall the Game. We hold no copy. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data, most requests are handled by the controls above or directly with Google. You can also contact us and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security and international transfers

Ad data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Google may process data in countries other than yours under appropriate safeguards described in its privacy policy.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be available in the Game and on our website with a new effective date.
